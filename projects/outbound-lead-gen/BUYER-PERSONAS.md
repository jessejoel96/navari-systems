## Layer One — Who We Contact

**Not:** anyone with a title match.  
**Yes:** a specific business where we can name **one visible problem** Navari fixes.

### Universal qualifying pattern (behavior, not sector)

A prospect qualifies when **most** of these are true:

| Signal | What it looks like |
|--------|-------------------|
| Spreadsheet ops | Shared Google Sheet/Excel built years ago still runs something critical |
| CRM underuse | Paid for CRM; ~30% adopted; rest lives in email |
| Human middleware | One person's memory + inbox is "the system" |
| Manual copy-paste | Form → spreadsheet → email → CRM by hand |
| Failed fix once | Tried automation; got busy; abandoned |
| Revenue band | **$150K–$2M/year** — pain is real, no full-time ops hire yet |
| **Highest-conversion signal** | Already paying a **freelancer, VA, or part-time hire** to bridge systems (data entry, bookkeeping, admin) |

**Unifying insight (adapt per industry):**
> *"We have a system — it's just mostly in someone's email and a spreadsheet they built three years ago."*

## Secondary personas — "The Outsourcer"

Businesses already paying humans for manual bridge work. Stack is usually **M365 + basic accounting + email**.

| Persona | Industry | Biggest visible leak | Job-board trigger keywords |
|---------|----------|---------------------|---------------------------|
| The Scaling Site Director | Construction / trades | Subcontractor invoice handling (10–15 hrs/wk) | bookkeeper part time, administrator Excel |
| The Multi-Site Owner | Retail (2–8 locations) | POS-to-accounting reconciliation | data entry, retail administrator |
| The Founding Director Still Doing Architecture | Architecture / engineering | Timesheet-to-invoice delay (15–20 days) | part time bookkeeper, admin assistant |
| The Operations Manager Doing Three Jobs | Logistics / freight | Manual client shipment status updates | operations administrator, data entry |
| MSP ops lead | IT MSPs | M365 license vs billing reconciliation | CRM data entry, administrator |
| Property dev ops | Property development | Lender reporting from multiple spreadsheets | bookkeeper, financial administrator |
| Staffing agency irony | Temp recruitment | Timesheet → payroll → invoice chain | payroll administrator, data entry |

**Job board outreach:** A posting for manual admin/data entry is a **published admission of the exact pain Navari solves**. Monitor Indeed, LinkedIn Jobs, Reed, Totaljobs for target industries + keywords above.

## Persona → observation starters

Use only after **15 minutes** of real research. These are **patterns to look for**, not copy-paste openers.

### Law — Stretched Partner
- *"Your contact form captures intake details, but your team site still routes new enquiries through a generic info@ inbox with no visible CRM handoff."*
- *"I couldn't find a single automated engagement or conflict-check step between 'contact us' and 'speak to a solicitor'."*

### Mortgage — Volume Broker
- *"Your rate/lead landing pages load fast, but there's no visible instant follow-up path — everything still looks like 'we'll call you back'."*
- *"Application checklist is PDF-based; nothing suggests document upload is connected to a pipeline."*

### Estate agency — Stretched Agency Director
- *"Listings on your site don't appear to sync with portal feeds in real time — several properties show stale status vs Rightmove/Zillow equivalents."*
- *"Valuation requests go to a form, but there's no visible automated nurture between enquiry and viewing booked."*

### Accounting — Compliance-First Practice Owner
- *"Client onboarding still looks like email + attachment chase rather than a single tracked portal."*
- *"You're hiring (or recently hired) for data entry / part-time admin — usually means the stack isn't connected."*

### Online coaching — Seven-Figure Founder
- *"Checkout works, but post-purchase path looks manual — no visible automated onboarding sequence between payment and first lesson."*
- *"High-ticket programme page exists, but CRM/email tool integration isn't evident from the public stack."*

### Recruitment — Billing-Focused Director
- *"Job ads are live, but candidate screening looks entirely manual — no scheduling or shortlist automation visible."*
